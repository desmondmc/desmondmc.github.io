Do Good

Maximise your life for Good

The road to paradise (on earth) is bottom up by cultivating goodness in yourself and others.

  • It’s easier to do good when you’re physically close to the people you’re doing good for
  • Self-sacrificing only up to your pace where you can “run forever” like a marathon. If you burn out you can’t do good.
  • Personal health is a means to an end. It’s your vehicle for good
  • Personal wealth is a means to an end. It’s a tool for a good
  • Know yourself. Do good at what you’re good at
  • Bear fruit: Empowering other people to do good is the biggest lever. Give opportunities and foster mentorship and ownership.
  • Stay humble because the road to hell is paved with good intentions.
    • The world is complex. The world is already pretty good. Prefer via negativa or small simple interventions.
  • Via negativa: prefer removing known errors, harms and complications rather than adding interventions or rules
  • Time is your friend. Be patient
  • Hevy is an incredible way to do a little good for a lot of people at a distance
